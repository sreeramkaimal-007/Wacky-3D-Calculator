# Wacky 3D Calculator — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

## 1. Local-First Calculation Data
All calculations, calculation history, memory registers (`MC`/`MR`/`M+`/`M-`), theme selections, button animation styles, and sound preferences are stored strictly locally on your device. We never collect, transmit, or sell your calculations or personal math data.

## 2. Minimal Permissions
The app requests only:
- `VIBRATE` — for optional tactile haptic feedback when tapping keys.
- `INTERNET` — solely to load Google AdMob interstitial ads.
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` — required by Android 13+ for the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

We never access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or personal messages.

## 3. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob)
Wacky 3D Calculator is 100% free with all sound packs and themes unlocked, supported solely by Google AdMob interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Where required by law (EEA, UK, and California), a consent dialog is shown before loading personalized ads.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Wacky 3D Calculator is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
